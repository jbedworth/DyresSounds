# Dyre's Sounds

The sounds and sound picker that Dyre's AddOns share, for World of Warcraft:
Forever (beta). It is a library: it does nothing on its own. Install it when
one of Dyre's AddOns asks for it.

## What it has

- **Dyre's Picks:** nine effect sounds that ship with it (fading, swoosh,
  vanish, and magical disappear sounds), first in every sound menu.
- **Dyre's Speed, Dyre's Cats, Dyre's Bears, and Dyre's Water:** 22 more, by
  theme: speed boosts and engines for travel spells, and meows, growls, roars,
  and splashes for the Druid's forms.
- **Dyre's Aggression:** 11 battle cries and shouts, for Charge and other
  Warrior moves.
- **Dyre's Gore, Dyre's Smacks, and Dyre's Blades:** 14 crit sounds, from
  slices and splats to slaps, kisses, and sword swings.
- **Dyre's Sax:** an alto sax scale, eight notes from B-flat up to the
  octave, for chimes that count up (combo points).
- **Dyre's Bassoon:** a quick comic bassoon climb, eight rising steps,
  for chimes that count up.
- **Dyre's Fel Sounds:** a heartbeat, a wet smash, a mud splat, and a deep
  demon growl, for dark crit themes.
- The game's interface sounds, imp laughs and chirps (not on Classic Era or
  Anniversary), and Text to Speech. The Cooldown Manager's alert sounds
  (Animals, Devices, Impacts, Instruments, Warcraft 2, Warcraft 3) are off
  for now: they played only some of the time, or not at all.
- A sound menu grouped by theme; choosing a sound plays it.

The AddOns that use it list it as an optional dependency, so they always load;
without it, they play no sounds and say once in chat what to install.

## Compatibility

For the Forever beta client 1.60.1 (Interface 16001). WowUp users
(2.24.0 or later) can install it from the repository URL; GitHub releases
carry the Forever metadata WowUp needs.
